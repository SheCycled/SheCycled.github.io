Built with Purpose

Transforming Environmental Waste To Dignity

SheCycled is a circular economy social enterprise in Jos, Nigeria working at the intersection of climate action, menstrual health, and sustainable innovation.

We transform agricultural waste, discarded banana stems, into biodegradable sanitary pads, creating a more affordable and sustainable alternative to conventional menstrual products while addressing period poverty among women and girls, especially those in underserved communities and internally displaced persons camps.

We also promote climate education through school visits across Plateau and Adamawa States Nigeria, educating young people about environmental protection, recycling, and responsible waste management. We have also created economic opportunities for rural farmers by sourcing agricultural waste and for women through sustainable production and distribution.

Our vision is to ensure that by 2030, woman and girl in underserved communities across middle belt, Nigeria have access to free or affordable menstrual products in schools and workplaces.
